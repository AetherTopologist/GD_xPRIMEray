## Reading Boundary

**What this is:** Core CLI bend-magnitude fixture artifacts and traversal-step records.

**What this is not:** a Godot comparison, pixel parity, physical validation, renderer equivalence, or proof.

Higher resolution means more rays through the same declared fixture, not a new physical model.

## Fixture Family Overview

- Family: `grin_radial_smoke_family_v1`
- Observer basis: origin `[0, 0, -2]`, forward `[0, 0, 1]`, up `[0, 1, 0]`, canonical FOV `60` degrees
- Channels: `bend_magnitude_metric`, `traversal_step_count`
- Status: 4 fixtures; 12/12 attempted ladder runs PASS; Extended tier deferred by policy

Radial GRIN smoke transport. Observer-facing bend-magnitude snapshots from a radial gradient-index field. The only Core-runnable fixture family at v3.3.
