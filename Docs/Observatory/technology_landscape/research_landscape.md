# Research Landscape

## Scope

This view describes research-support capabilities around evidence capture, reproducibility, validation, and communication. It does not certify scientific validity or research readiness.

## Research Contexts

| Context | Capability vocabulary | Project Glowing Heart evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Reproducible computational experiments | fixtures, versions, manifests, deterministic inputs, retained outputs | Baseline and artifact records begin in [v0.1](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v0_1_baseline.md) and [v0.1 artifact](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v0_1_artifact.md). |
| Observable simulation systems | machine-readable metrics, human-readable summaries, visual artifacts | Observable output and first human-facing evidence are documented in [v0.3](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v0_3_observable_output.md) and [v0.6](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v0_6_first_human_observable.md). |
| Validation frameworks | explicit hypotheses, acceptance conditions, limitations, negative results | The Glowing Heart gap matrix records known differences in [v1.2](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v1_2_gap_matrix.md); no broad validation conclusion follows from it. |
| Comparative visualization | aligned observations, declared channels, transforms, difference encodings | Observer alignment exists at preview-contract level in [v1.8](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v1_8_milestone.md); measurement comparability is constrained by [v1.9](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v1_9_shared_snapshot_measurement_contract.md). |
| Public research communication | claim boundaries, evidence links, demo readiness, revision history | Public-demo claim boundaries are documented in [v1.5](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_v1_5_public_demo_readiness.md). |

General reference contexts include [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/), [Software Heritage identifiers](https://docs.softwareheritage.org/devel/swh-model/persistent-identifiers.html), and [Jupyter reproducibility guidance](https://jupyter.org/). These references provide vocabulary for evidence stewardship; they do not confer compliance or validation status.

## Current Boundary

The current trail supports a documented prototype with growing evidence infrastructure. Research Ready requires independently reviewable methods, stable fixtures, provenance-complete artifacts, uncertainty and failure reporting, and replicated results. Those gates are not yet evidenced for Project Glowing Heart as a whole.
