# 10 Biological Observers

## Scope

Biological observers convert environmental signals into organism-specific responses. Relevant examples include the human eye, retina, photoreceptors, compound eyes, polarization-sensitive vision, echolocation, and other sensory systems.

## Observation Layers

| Layer | Atlas question |
|---|---|
| Collection | Which wavelengths, directions, or signal forms reach the organism? |
| Transduction | How does tissue convert incoming energy into biological signals? |
| Adaptation | How do sensitivity and response change with context or time? |
| Encoding | Which spatial, temporal, color, or categorical features are retained? |
| Interpretation | Which perceptual account is being presented, and with what uncertainty? |

## Relevance to xPRIMEray

Biological observation motivates explicit receiver models and reminds computational systems that a rendered pixel is not perception itself. Future observer declarations may describe spectral response, acuity, field of view, adaptation, or sampling, but no biological equivalence is currently asserted.

Project Glowing Heart provides the emerging protocol layer for describing fixtures, observers, snapshots, measurements, artifacts, and future comparisons. Biological models would require extensions beyond the current preview camera and measurement contracts.
