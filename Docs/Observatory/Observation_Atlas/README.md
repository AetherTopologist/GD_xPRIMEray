# Observation Atlas

A field guide to observation systems — from human vision and laboratory instruments to computational cameras and xPRIMEray.

The Atlas is a map of observation systems, not a ranking of technologies. It describes what each kind of observer *receives*, *transforms*, *measures*, and *preserves*, without treating them as interchangeable.

The [Atlas Constitution](ATLAS_CONSTITUTION.md) states the reading rules for this map. The [Observer Grammar](observer_grammar.md) and [Representation Grammar](representation_grammar.md) are the shared forms used to describe entries consistently.

## Why this exists

Every observation — retinal, detector, or simulated — follows a chain: something arrives, something records it, something is stored, and only then may someone interpret it.

xPRIMEray is building vocabulary and evidence for that chain in software. This Atlas places that work on a wider chart so biological, optical, scientific, computational, and educational observers can be compared *structurally*, not collapsed into one "ground truth."

## Relationship to Project Glowing Heart

Glowing Heart is the engine-heart protocol program. The Atlas is the map.

Glowing Heart documents fixtures, observers, snapshots, and comparison readiness inside this repository. The Atlas does not duplicate that trail; it shows the surrounding observer families that inform it. Its evidence trail is indexed in the [Glowing Heart Atlas link note](../../xPRIMEray/project_glowing_heart_atlas_link.md).

## Reading boundary

- The Atlas does not rank technologies or claim that one observer family is "more true" than another.
- No parity or pixel-equivalence claim between systems unless a documented transform and evidence are cited.
- No physics-validation claim from visuals or simulations alone.
- Educational and artistic views are interpretive — useful for orientation, not scientific proof.
