# 20 Optical Observers

## Scope

Optical observers shape and collect light before detection. The family includes lenses, mirrors, apertures, microscopes, telescopes, fiber systems, gradient-index media, and compound optical assemblies.

## Observation Layers

| Layer | Capability description |
|---|---|
| Medium | Declares refractive, reflective, absorptive, scattering, or gradient-index behavior |
| Geometry | Declares surfaces, distances, apertures, axes, and alignment |
| Transport | Describes how rays or fields evolve through the assembly |
| Receiver | Describes image plane, sensor, eyepiece, or other detector interface |
| Measurement | Declares units, normalization, dynamic range, and uncertainty |
